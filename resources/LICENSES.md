## Shader References

BBFx shaders are original implementations. Some are inspired by well-known techniques:
- Perlin noise — Based on Ken Perlin's gradient noise algorithm (public domain technique)
- Mandelbrot/Julia sets — Mathematical formulas (public domain)
- Various post-process effects — Standard computer graphics techniques
